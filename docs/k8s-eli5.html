Kubernetes, explained like you're five

It sounds scary. It isn't. It's just a very tireless robot that runs an apartment building full of tiny apps — using our own little shop (microecom) as the example.

🎛️ The one big idea

Kubernetes (people write it k8s — K, then 8 letters, then s) is a wish list, not a to-do list.

You don't tell it every little step. You say: “I want 3 copies of my shop running, always.” Then tireless robots keep checking reality and fixing it until it matches your wish — forever. A app crashed at 3am? The robots already replaced it before you woke up. 💤
