# Motion — mechanical, not smooth

```css
--press-translate: 4px;
--transition-snap: 60ms steps(2);
```

`steps(2)` is the signature. Buttons translate `4px 4px` down-right on `:active`, and the
shadow shrinks to `--shadow-sm`. The two-step transition feels like a printing press
impact, not Material's eased ripple — a smooth `ease-out` curve would read as a rubbery
UI toolkit, not a stamped object hitting paper.

## Press it

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Spot} />

Click and hold the button above — watch it translate and its shadow shrink in two
discrete steps rather than a smooth glide.

## Reduced motion

The brutalist press is fun but not essential. Respect
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` — see the a11y checklist
(`docs/09-a11y-checklist.md`, mirrored as **Guides/A11y** in this Storybook once
published).
