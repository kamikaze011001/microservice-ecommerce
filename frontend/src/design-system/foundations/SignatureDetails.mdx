# Signature Details — Issue Nº01

These seven details are codified in the design spec (`docs/02-design-tokens.md` §"Signature
details"). Use them — they're what makes Issue Nº01 not generic neo-brutalism.

## 1. Stamps, not badges

`<BStamp>` is used for status (PROCESSING / PAID / CANCELED): a double-ring border,
condensed mono type, `--stamp-red`, and a slight rotation — not a rounded pill badge.

<Canvas of={StampStories.Paid} />

## 2. Misregistration on hover

Product card titles get `text-shadow: 2px 2px 0 var(--spot)` on hover. It looks like the
red and orange print passes slipped out of alignment for a moment.

<Canvas of={SignatureStories.HoverMisregister} />

Hover the card above — watch the heading pick up the offset shadow.

## 3. Marginalia numerals

`<BMarginNumeral>` renders huge outlined section numerals ("01", "02") in the page margin.

<Canvas of={MarginNumeralStories.Left} />

## 4. Cropmark dividers

`<BCropmarks>` replaces `<hr>` with four small black corner marks — a print-shop
registration mark, not a line. Note `BCropmarks` has no slot; it's a fixed divider that
sits between blocks, not a wrapper around content.

<Canvas of={CropmarksStories.Default} />

## 5. Sticker rotation

Product cards sit at ±0.5° random rotation — pinned-to-a-wall, not machine-aligned to a grid.

<Canvas of={SignatureStories.StickerRotation} />

## 6. Paper grain

The page background carries a subtle SVG noise overlay at ~4% opacity, applied via
`body::after` in `main.css`:

```css
body::after {
  content: '';
  position: fixed;
  inset: 0;
  pointer-events: none;
  opacity: 0.04;
  background-image: url('data:image/svg+xml;utf8,...fractalNoise...');
  mix-blend-mode: multiply;
}
```

There's no isolated embed for this one — it's a full-page effect. It's visible on every
story canvas in this Storybook as a faint texture over the paper background, softening the
otherwise flat two-tone fills.

## 7. The CTA press

Buttons translate `4px 4px` down-right on `:active` and their shadow shrinks from
`--shadow-md` to `--shadow-sm`, using the `60ms steps(2)` transition — a printing-press
impact rather than a smooth Material ripple. See **Foundations/Motion** for the live,
interactive demo of this signature.
